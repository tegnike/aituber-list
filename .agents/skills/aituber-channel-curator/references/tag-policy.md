# AITuber tag policy

Use the repository's Japanese tag names exactly. Do not invent synonyms.

| Tag | Apply when | Do not apply when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `コメント応答` | The AI character autonomously responds to live-chat comments, or repeated live content clearly shows comment/viewer participation as a core interaction. | The description merely says “chat” without evidence of AI response or viewer interaction. |
| `解説` | The channel repeatedly publishes explanatory, tutorial, educational, news-analysis, weather, horoscope, or reference-style content. | A single casual mention of a topic, a pure music video, or ordinary conversation. |
| `歌唱あり` | Singing streams, karaoke, recurring singing content, or a clear body of song-performance videos exists. | The description only says singing is possible, a music video uses AI-generated vocals without the character singing, or a song is mentioned as a request. |
| `海外` | The channel is demonstrably operated primarily for a non-Japanese audience or its content is primarily in a non-Japanese language. Require repeated non-Japanese content, a clearly overseas-oriented channel identity, or equivalent evidence. | An occasional English title, bilingual “international fans welcome” notice, translated metadata, or English chat support on an otherwise Japanese channel. |
| `ゲーム実況` | Repeated gameplay commentary or game-streaming sessions are present. | A game is mentioned in the profile, a game-related short is not gameplay, or a one-off game announcement has no play session. |
| `AIパートナー` | AI participates as a partner to a human streamer or human-led channel. | The AI character is the channel's sole/main streamer, or another AI character is merely a guest. |
| `複数キャラ` | Two or more recurring AI characters are central to the channel's content. | A guest, collaborator, or one-off alternate costume is the only additional character. |
| `一部AITuber` | Human-led or ordinary content is primary and an AI character appears only in part of the content. | An AITuber character is the main subject, even when the channel also has human production credits. |

## Special classification rule

`一部AITuber` is a classification tag, not a normal content filter. Preserve it internally when applicable, but follow the repository UI's rule that it is excluded from the ordinary tag selector and handled by the dedicated “AITuber main only” filter.

## Evidence standard

Prefer a combination of the channel description and repeated titles across videos, streams, and shorts. A tag may be applied from one especially clear item when the item directly proves the behavior (for example, a stream titled “viewer participation” or a tutorial titled “how to use ...”), but explain that evidence in the report. When evidence is ambiguous, omit the tag rather than over-classifying.
